Zeppelin Design Labs now offers two professional contact microphones. Both deliver balanced XLR output and phantom power operation, but they target different workflows.

## Cortado Xe — compact and magnet-friendly

The [Cortado Xe](/products/cortado-xe/) integrates sensor, preamp, and cable into a **26mm × 11mm** all-steel housing. It is ideal for:

- Acoustic guitar, violin, harp, and other instruments
- Luthier voicing and temporary evaluation
- Any application where low profile and magnet mounting matter

Frequency response: approximately **40 Hz – 40 kHz**.

## Cortado MkIII — rugged and modular

The [Cortado MkIII](/products/cortado-mkiii/) separates the sensor from a steel chassis housing the electronics. Switchable **pad** and **bass boost** extend versatility. Choose MkIII when you need:

- Proven durability in heat, cold, and physical stress
- Long cable runs with a balanced low-noise signal
- Field recording and demanding installations

Frequency response: approximately **23 Hz – 40 kHz** with bass boost on.
